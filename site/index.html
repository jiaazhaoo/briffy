不需要录入和整理的笔记本。

A notebook you don’t have to write or tidy.

不需要录入

No writing

收藏、截图、复制的东西自动记下来；任意文件拖给它就行。

Saves, screenshots and copies are recorded on their own — and you can drop any file on it.

不需要整理

No tidying

所有内容自动转成文本，自动归成主题、写好摘要。

Everything becomes text, then groups itself into topics with the summaries written for you.

一句话出一份报告

A report in one sentence

调研报告、行程单、材料表——说一句你要什么，它只读你自己的记录来写，每句标着第几条。

A research write-up, an itinerary, a parts list — say what you need. It writes only from your own records, and every line cites the one it came from.

macOS · Windows · recognition runs locally · source-available, noncommercial

收藏从未停止，学习从未开始

Saving never stops. Reading never starts.

收藏焦虑、收集癖、松鼠症、数字囤积、稍后再读、收藏夹吃灰——名字很多，症状只有一个：你收的东西，一次都没回去看过。

Digital hoarding, collector’s itch, the bookmark graveyard, read-it-later, tab hoarding — many names, one symptom: you have never gone back to any of it.

不是你懒。是你存下的只是一个链接，而链接会失效、会要登录、会被改掉；而且它从来没被归到任何地方去。

It isn’t laziness. What you saved was a link — and links rot, ask for a login, get rewritten. And it was never filed anywhere.

briffy 存的是整篇正文，自动归成主题、写好摘要，还能一句话问出来。收藏夹不用再吃灰。

briffy keeps the whole article, groups it by topic, writes the summary, and answers questions out of it. The graveyard turns back into a notebook.

扔什么都行

Throw anything in

不用建文件夹，不用起名字，不用打标签，也没有「新建记录」这个按钮。

No folders, no names, no tags — and no “new entry” button.

任意社媒的收藏，连正文一起

Any site’s bookmarks, body and all

X 的 Bookmark、Reddit 的 Save、小红书和知乎的收藏。存下来的是整篇正文，不是一个链接——而且是在页面里抽的，所以要登录才看得见的东西也存得下来。

X’s Bookmark, Reddit’s Save, and the rest. The whole article is archived, not a link — and it’s extracted inside the page, so what only shows up once you’re logged in comes too.

会议自己录下来

Meetings record themselves

它不听房间，它跟着麦克风：别的软件打开麦克风，它才录（腾讯会议、Zoom、Teams、Meet⋯⋯）。录完转成文字，还分得出说话人 1 / 2 / 3。默认关着。

It doesn’t listen to the room, it follows the microphone: it records only when another app opens it (Zoom, Teams, Meet, Tencent Meeting…). Then it’s transcribed and split into speaker 1 / 2 / 3. Off by default.

常驻剪贴板

It lives on your clipboard

你按 CtrlC 的每一次都落进来——文字、图片、文件。同一次复制只算一条，不会因为格式多就存好几份。

Every CtrlC lands: text, images, files. One copy is one record — several clipboard flavours of the same thing don’t become several.

拖什么进来都行

Drop anything on it

图片、PDF、音频、视频、代码、一段选中的文字。超过 200 MB 的不复制，只记住它在哪儿。

Images, PDFs, audio, video, code, a selected paragraph. Anything over 200 MB isn’t copied — it just remembers where it lives.

任意格式变成文本

Anything becomes text

截图里的字、录音里的话、PDF、网页正文——全部变成能搜的文字，识别在本机。

The words inside a screenshot, the words inside a recording, a PDF, a page’s body — all of it becomes searchable text, recognised on your own machine.

想主动记一笔也行：点它截屏、双击框选、长按说一句，或按 CtrlAlt + A / S / V（macOS 上 CmdShift）。

You can also act on purpose: click it to capture, double-click for a region, hold to speak — or CtrlAlt + A / S / V (CmdShift on macOS).

然后它自己成堆

Then it piles itself up

讲同一件事的草稿会聚到一起。本子上于是有了一条竖着的线索，不再只是按天排的一条流。

Drafts about the same thing gather together, so a thread runs down the notebook instead of one stream sorted by day.

归堆完全在本地，不经过模型——向量本来就在索引里，两百次点积，毫秒级。只有给一堆起名字才用一次 AI。

The grouping is entirely local and never touches a model — the vectors are already in the index, and it is a couple of hundred dot products. Only naming a pile costs one AI call.

一张草稿长什么样

What a draft looks like

五种草稿，五种纸。认它靠轮廓，不靠图标。标题和那句摘要也是软件写的。

Five kinds of draft, five kinds of paper — told apart by outline, not icons. The title and the one-line summary are written for you too.

本子得有出口

A notebook needs a way out

只进不出的本子，就是另一个收藏夹。一句话问它，答案标着依据的第几条。

A notebook you only put things into is just another bookmarks folder. Ask in a sentence; every claim carries the draft it came from.

你的 AI 读得到

Your AI can read it

一个只读的 MCP 服务器：Claude、Cursor、VS Code、Codex 接上就能查你的日常上下文。它直接读工作区文件，briffy 开没开都行。

A read-only MCP server: Claude, Cursor, VS Code and Codex can query your day. It reads the workspace files directly, so it answers whether or not briffy is running.

每天早上，昨天已经写好了

Yesterday is written by morning

到设定时间（默认 08:00）昨天的摘要自己写好，弹一句通知。你没有要求过它。

At the hour you set (08:00 by default) yesterday’s summary has written itself, and says so. You never asked for it.

它凭什么敢一直开着

Why it can be always on

因为它不按时间记，按动作记。你不动手的时候，它什么都不做——这不是承诺，是它做不到。

Because it records by what you do, not by the clock. When you are not doing anything, neither is it — that is not a promise, it is what the mechanism allows.

不监视浏览It doesn't watch you browse

浏览器扩展平时什么都不做，只有一次保存手势发生之后才去读页面。

The extension does nothing until a save gesture has actually happened; only then does it read the page.

识别在本机Recognition is local

文字识别和语音转写都是本地引擎，装完断网也能用。一张 2560×1440 的截图约 0.8 秒。

Text and speech recognition are local engines; unplug the network and they still run. About 0.8 s for a 2560×1440 screenshot.

东西在你自己的硬盘上It lives on your own disk

一天一个索引文件，原图、原音、原文件在旁边。没有云，没有账号。

One index file per day, with the original images, audio and files beside it. No cloud, no account.

会离开这台电脑的What does leave

只有软件替你写的字：标题、摘要、问的作答。交给你选的 AI 服务。不配也能用。

Only the words written for you: titles, summaries, answers — sent to the AI service you pick. Configure nothing and it still runs.

装它

Install

下载、拖进「应用程序」、打开。没有账号，不用注册，不用配置——第一次启动它自己检查这台电脑，把本机的文字识别和语音识别准备好。

Download it, drag it to Applications, open it. No account, no sign-up, nothing to configure — on first launch it checks this machine and gets local text and speech recognition ready by itself.
