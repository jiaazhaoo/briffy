隐私政策

Privacy Policy

briffy 没有服务器。你的截图、录音、剪贴板和文件都留在你自己的电脑上；只有你自己配置了 AI 服务或外部连接时，被处理的那一条记录才会离开这台机器，直接发给你自己的账号。

briffy has no server. Your screenshots, recordings, clipboard and files stay on your own computer. Something leaves this machine only when you have configured an AI service or an external connection yourself — and then it goes directly to your own account with that provider, never to us.

生效日期 2026-09-08 · 版本 1.0 · 适用于 briffy 桌面端（macOS / Windows）和 briffy 浏览器扩展

Effective 8 September 2026 · Version 1.0 · Covers the briffy desktop app (macOS / Windows) and the briffy browser extension

自动录音，单独说

Automatic listening, stated plainly

这是 briffy 唯一一件不问就做的事，所以把它讲全。

This is the one thing briffy does without being asked, so here it is in full.

默认关闭。不打开它，麦克风只在你主动开始一段语音笔记时打开。

It is off by default. Leave it off and the microphone opens only while you are recording a voice note.

在 设置 › 录音 里打开之后，briffy 会一直持有麦克风，只要有人说话就存一段录音，不需要你按任何键。于是会议和通话会进工作区，不管当时有没有人打算记它，包括房间里其他人的声音。只要它在跑，macOS 就一直显示橙色的麦克风指示点。

With it on (Settings › Recording), briffy holds the microphone and files a recording whenever somebody speaks — no keypress needed. Meetings and calls end up in the workspace whether or not anyone meant them to, other people’s voices included. macOS shows the orange microphone indicator the whole time it runs.

录音和转写依然只写在本机。但转写出来的文字会和其它记录一样，在你配了云端 AI 服务时被送去写标题和摘要。

Recordings and their transcripts still never leave your machine. The transcribed text, though, is treated like any other record: with a cloud AI service configured, it is sent off to have a title and summary written.

录音别人是否合法，取决于你在哪里。有些国家和地区要求所有被录的人都同意。这一层 briffy 不能替你判断，也不替你承担；开这个开关之前请自己确认。

Whether recording other people is lawful depends on where you are. Some jurisdictions require everyone’s consent. briffy cannot make that judgement for you and does not carry it for you; please check before turning this on.

什么会离开这台电脑

What leaves this machine

只有你自己开启的功能才会联网。默认安装、什么都不配的情况下，briffy 除了下载它需要的本地模型之外不发送任何东西。

Only features you turn on yourself use the network. Installed and left unconfigured, briffy sends nothing at all beyond fetching the local models it needs.

AI 服务（写标题、摘要、回答提问）The AI service (titles, summaries, answers)

你在 设置 › AI 服务 里四选一。选定之后，被处理的那一条记录的内容会发给该服务：截图图片本身、OCR 文字、PDF 正文、抓取到的网页正文，以及你在「问」里输入的问题和被检索到的记录摘录。

You pick one of four in Settings › AI service. From then on, the content of the record being processed is sent there: the screenshot image itself, its OCR text, PDF or web-page text, plus the questions you type in Ask and excerpts of the records it retrieved.

这是转发，不是我们收集。briffy 不保存、不代理、也看不到这些请求——它们从你的电脑直连服务商，用的是你自己的凭据。不想让任何内容离开本机，就选 Ollama，或者干脆不配：不配时程序照常可用，标题退回文件名，摘要退化为清单，「问」这一页仍然能在本地检索。

This is forwarding, not collection on our part. briffy neither stores nor proxies these requests and cannot see them — they go straight from your computer to the provider under your own credentials. Want nothing to leave? Choose Ollama, or configure nothing: titles fall back to filenames, summaries to a list, and Ask still searches locally.

浏览器扩展

The browser extension

扩展只和同一台电脑上的 briffy 说话（127.0.0.1:47831）。它没有自己的服务器，也不向任何第三方发送东西。

The extension talks only to briffy on the same computer (127.0.0.1:47831). It has no server of its own and sends nothing to any third party.

你勾选的媒体Media you tick
面板里勾中的图片、视频、音频，交给本机的 briffyThe images, video and audio you select in the panel, handed to briffy locally
你收藏的页面Pages you save
你按了网页自己的「收藏」时，那一页连正文一起存进 briffyWhen you press a site’s own save button, that page and its text are kept in briffy
媒体请求的地址Media request URLs
只看经过的地址，用来找出 DOM 里看不见的视频流。不拦截、不改写、不读响应内容URLs are observed only, to find streams the page never puts in its markup. Nothing is blocked or rewritten, and no response body is ever read
当前页的地址和标题The active page’s URL and title
默认关闭。只有 briffy 明确要求时才发（让存下来的东西知道自己来自哪儿）Off by default. Sent only when briffy explicitly asks, so a saved item knows where it came from
当前页的正文The active page’s text
默认关闭。只有你在 briffy 里打开「不用动手存的那一层」之后才发Off by default. Sent only after you turn on briffy’s “no-hands” layer

后两项默认是关着的：扩展启动时它们都是 false，要等本机的 briffy 在回应里明确要求才会打开——也就是说 briffy 必须正在运行，而且那个设置是你自己开的。

The last two start switched off: both flags are false when the extension starts, and turn on only if briffy’s reply explicitly asks for them — which means briffy has to be running with a setting you turned on yourself.

扩展不监视浏览：认收藏那一层平时什么都不做，只有一次保存手势真的发生之后才去读页面。它不需要账号，也不存任何东西到云上。

The extension does not watch you browse: the save-detection layer sits still and reads the page only after a save gesture has actually happened. It needs no account and stores nothing in any cloud.

端口可以在 设置 里改，也可以把这个本机接口整个关掉。

The port is configurable in Settings, and the whole local endpoint can be switched off.

我们不做什么

What briffy does not do

没有遥测、没有分析、没有崩溃上报。代码里没有任何统计 SDK；源码是公开的，这一条可以自己 grep 验证。

No telemetry, no analytics, no crash reporting. There is no analytics SDK anywhere in the code; the source is public, so you can grep for it yourself.

没有 briffy 的服务器。上面所有目的地都是第三方或本机，没有一个是我们的。

There is no briffy server. Every destination above is either a third party or your own machine. None of them is ours.

不做广告、不做画像、不追踪、不卖数据、不与任何人共享。也不因为「同步」而上传——briffy 不做云同步。

No advertising, no profiling, no tracking, no selling, no sharing with anyone. And nothing is uploaded for “sync” — briffy does not sync to a cloud.
